🏝️ More than 30,000 islands: Lake Huron's islands contribute to its exceptionally long shoreline.
🌎 Manitoulin Island: It is the world's largest island in a freshwater lake.
🇺🇸 🇨🇦 International lake: Lake Huron is shared by Michigan and Ontario.
📊 Great Lakes ranking: Lake Huron is the second-largest Great Lake by surface area and third-largest by water volume.
🌊 Major waters: Georgian Bay and Saginaw Bay are two of Lake Huron's best-known large bays.
⏳ Water retention: Lake Huron's water-retention time is about 22 years.
